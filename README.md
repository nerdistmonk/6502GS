This project was an attempt at a merlin-like assembler for Apple II family computers, however I decided to stop development due to a lack of faith and confidence in my work since this is too far outside of my normal wheelhouse.

Design.md shows the current implemented design and open items, I walked away because I felt the software was too complex and inconsistent due to it's design being based on notes and documents rather than anything concrete.

It was routinely passing the tests included in the test folder, so it may be better than I give it credit for, but I just got no desire left to continue work on this.

Leaving the codebase here for anyone else who might see use for it.
